# Glide Mouse Troubleshooting FAQ

If Glide does not connect, check AA battery polarity, switch the mouse off and on, connect the USB-A receiver, and try another port. If the computer has only USB-C, use a compatible adapter. Glide uses a 2.4 GHz receiver, not Bluetooth.

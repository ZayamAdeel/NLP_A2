# PocketBeat Speaker Pairing FAQ

Charge PocketBeat, turn it on and activate pairing mode, open Bluetooth settings, select PocketBeat, then test audio at low volume. If pairing fails, move devices closer, toggle Bluetooth, and remove an obsolete pairing. It supports one active Bluetooth audio connection.

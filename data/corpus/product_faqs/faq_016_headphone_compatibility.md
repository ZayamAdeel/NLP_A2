# WaveSound H200 Compatibility FAQ

Q: Can I use H200 with my laptop and phone? A: Yes, if the device supports Bluetooth audio. Pair through the device Bluetooth settings. USB-C is for charging, not wired audio. If pairing fails, charge the headphones, remove an old pairing if necessary, and retry pairing mode.

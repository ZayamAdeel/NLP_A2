# ClearView Webcam Setup FAQ

Connect the C920 to USB-A, open the video-call app and select it as camera/microphone. Grant camera and microphone permissions. If it is missing, reconnect it, try another port, close apps using the camera and check privacy settings. Maximum video is 1080p at 30 fps.

# LinkHub HDMI Troubleshooting FAQ

HDMI requires the host USB-C port to support video output, such as DisplayPort Alt Mode or compatible Thunderbolt. A data-only USB-C port may not output video. Check host specifications, display input and HDMI cable. Maximum listed output is 4K at 30 Hz on compatible hosts.

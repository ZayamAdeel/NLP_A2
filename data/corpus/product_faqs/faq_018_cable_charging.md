# FlexCharge Charging-Speed FAQ

Q: Does the cable charge every phone at 60 W? A: No. The cable is rated up to 60 W, but the device, charger and charging protocol must also support that power. USB 2.0 describes data transfer, not charging speed. Do not use cables with damaged insulation.
